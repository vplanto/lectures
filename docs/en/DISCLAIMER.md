## 0. Author Declaration

Portal materials are an **evolution of the author's ideas**, not publication of finished presentations as-is.

The basic content, concept, and architecture of the courses were developed by the author based on their own lectures and presentations. Large language model (LLM) tools were used exclusively as a technical means for format conversion (Slides → Markdown) and expanding textual theses. All final text, architectural diagrams, and code were fully reviewed, verified, and approved by the author.

---

## 1. Regulatory Framework

Educational and research materials on this portal align with **[Ukrainian Law No. 4742-IX «On Higher Education»](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/4742-IX)** regarding academic integrity, including:

- originality of authored materials;
- correct citation and attribution of primary sources;
- transparent use of artificial intelligence (AI) tools;
- shared responsibility of students and instructors for learning outcomes and assessment.

The hub's full register of regulatory and methodological references is in [`sources.md`](./sources.md).

---

## 2. Use of Artificial Intelligence

On author-led content design and LLM role in format conversion — see [Section 0](#0-author-declaration). Below — the iterative development protocol and rules for students.

Course materials may be developed or extended using AI assistants under the **iterative protocol** described in:

**→ [Protocol for Developing High-Volume Technical Lectures](./10_ai_lectures.md)**

This protocol defines AI as a **methodological tool**, not a substitute for engineering expertise. It is **not duplicated** in individual lectures — lectures link to it instead.

**Students must not:**
- submit AI-generated work as fully their own without declaration;
- use AI during exams or graded assessments when explicitly prohibited;
- cite AI-generated text without fact-checking and primary sources.

**Students may (with declaration):**
- use AI to explain concepts, refactor their own code, or debug;
- apply tools from [methodological materials](./04_notebooklm_manual.md) for self-study.

## 4. Sources and Citation

- Primary sources (papers, specifications, standards) are collected in `{course}/sources.md` as `[1]…[N]` — **links only**, no excerpted text.
- Inline links remain for: demo repositories, YouTube, NotebookLM, Cursor, and similar learning tools.
- Plagiarism, data fabrication, and misappropriation of code are treated as academic integrity violations per institutional policy.
