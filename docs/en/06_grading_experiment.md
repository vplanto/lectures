# Grading Regulation: "Risk & Reward" Model

> 🇺🇦 [Українська версія](../06_grading_experiment.md) | 🇬🇧 English version

## General Provisions
This document defines the procedure for final assessment control. The system is based on principles of transparency, voluntariness, and personal responsibility for results. The model's goal is to ensure objective knowledge assessment and provide motivated students an opportunity to confirm high qualification level.

## 1. Grading Structure

Final grade process consists of two stages: **Cumulative (mandatory)** and **Corrective (voluntary)**.

### Stage 1: Point Accumulation (Low Risk)
Basis of rating is systematic work throughout semester.
* **Points source:** Practical assignments completion (Lab/Home assignments).
* **Maximum result:** 100 points.
* **Principle:** Conscientious assignment completion guarantees positive grade (60–100 points). Taking exam to pass/graduate **is not mandatory** if student is satisfied with current rating.

### Stage 2: Exam (High Risk — High Reward)
This stage is intended exclusively for rating improvement. Participation is student's conscious choice.
* **Admission condition:** Minimum passing score from practical work.
* **Improvement limit:** Maximum **+20 points** to current result.
* **Format:** Choosing ticket of appropriate difficulty category.

## 2. Question Categorization and Value

Questions are classified by difficulty level. Student independently chooses risk level before ticket selection.

| Tier | Competencies Assessed | Outcome (Reward/Risk) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **I. Basic** | Fundamental knowledge: syntax, definitions, reproducing simple algorithms. | **+/- 5 points** |
| **II. Advanced** | Understanding working mechanisms, comparative technology analysis, finding and fixing code errors. | **+/- 10 points** |
| **III. Expert** | System design, architectural decisions, optimization, edge-case handling. | **+/- 20 points** |

*Table compiled based on source data.*

### Exam Procedure
1.  **Declaration:** Student announces desired difficulty level (Tier I, II, or III).
2.  **Answer:** Student answers ticket questions (theory and practice).
3.  **Result:**
    * **Complete answer:** Points added to rating.
    * **Incorrect answer:** Points **deducted** from rating.
    * **Incomplete answer:** 0 points (current result preserved) or partial point deduction at instructor's discretion.
4.  **Attempt:** Only one grade improvement attempt allowed.

## 3. Methodology Justification

This system is implemented to achieve the following goals:

1.  **Self-Assessment Development:** Requires student skill in adequately assessing own competence (Seniority awareness), critical for engineers.
2.  **Academic Integrity:** Point loss mechanism makes "guessing" or cheating economically disadvantageous. High grade confirms real, deep knowledge.
3.  **Efficiency:** Students who completed minimum program are freed from session stress. Instructor focuses attention on examining students claiming highest grades ("A" / "Excellent").

**Student Warning:**
Choosing **Tier III** level, you confirm readiness to solve complex engineering tasks. Be careful when assessing own strengths: uncertainty or attempted luck can significantly lower your final score.
